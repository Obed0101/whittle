Build `site/pricing.html`, the pricing page for Relay, our team chat product. One self-contained file: inline CSS and JavaScript, nothing loaded from the network.

I want it to feel premium, not a bare table.

Plans: Free ($0), Team ($8 per user a month) and Business ($15 per user a month). Yearly billing is 20% cheaper; let people switch between monthly and yearly and see the prices change. Team is the one we want people to pick, so make it stand out. Each plan has a short feature list and a call-to-action button.

Below the plans, a comparison table of features across the three plans, and an FAQ with at least four questions that open and close.

It has to look right on phones, support dark mode, have tasteful motion (and respect people who turn motion off), and be usable with a keyboard and a screen reader.
